- **Source of truth:** FlowFret frontend routes, English translations, chord data (`src/data/guitar/guitar.json`) and music/player controls. Backend was not running during the earlier environment check; do not show server-backed track results, user activity, or counts.

- **Brand:** supplied FlowFret mark, charcoal surfaces, rose `#E11D48`, pearl text. Use Cinetic’s bundled sans faces.
- **Sound:** licensed Mixkit music and recorded effects only; keep license records private in the project.
- **Hard bans:** no invented songs, lyrics, users, metrics, timestamps, playback analysis, or guaranteed provider results; no changes to FlowFret business code; no final-promo production files outside this folder.
