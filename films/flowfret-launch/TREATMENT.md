# FlowFret — launch film

## Idea

Six tuned strings become one clear C-major shape, then the shape becomes a practice loop. The interface and every chord value come from FlowFret’s real chord library and player controls.

**Cinetic direction:** wall-inspired, adapted to FlowFret's five real chord results rather than invented screens. The chosen `wall` direction was outside the first dealt options because it best carried the verified result list into the product's A/B loop; the film keeps FlowFret's charcoal and rose palette and the requested 50-second runtime.

**Idea sentence:** FlowFret's C-major voicing diagram turns into its A/B practice loop.

**Competitor test:** A chord reference without FlowFret's own A/B loop could not complete this exact UI-to-practice transition.

The A/B controls arrive as part of the real player practice-loop UI. A rising wall of FlowFret’s actual tool labels surrounds the focused chord before the camera returns to the supplied mark.
